# 🌱 CropConnect – Field To Door Produce

**Fresh from Farmers, Direct to You.**

## 📌 About the Project
CropConnect is a digital farmer-to-customer marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers. It helps farmers sell freshly harvested vegetables while allowing customers to purchase fresh produce conveniently.

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Farmers often depend on intermediaries to sell their produce, reducing their profits. Customers may also find it difficult to know the source and freshness of the vegetables they purchase.

## 💡 Our Solution
CropConnect provides a digital platform where farmers can list their fresh vegetables directly. Customers can browse available produce, view farmer information, select quantities, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries.

## ✨ Key Features
- Direct farmer-to-customer connection
- Fresh vegetable listings
- Farmer information
- Product quantity selection
- Online ordering
- Payment functionality
- Delivery tracking

## 🔄 Working Flow

**Farmer → CropConnect → Customer → Delivery**

## 🎯 Objective
To reduce dependence on intermediaries, support farmers, and make fresh vegetables accessible to customers.
